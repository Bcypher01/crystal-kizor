04 — The person

Hello, I'm Crystal.

Designer, builder, teacher, mother of movements.

Design Director and Lead Principal of Studio COKA — architect of Nigeria's first fully off-grid hospital. 100,000+ sqm designed and coordinated, always asking how a building reduces energy, raises living standards and belongs to its climate.

  • B.Sc Architecture — University of Nigeria, Nsukka
  • MA Interior Architecture & Design — Coventry University
  • Cert. Sustainable Real Estate — University of Cambridge
